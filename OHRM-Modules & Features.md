## 🌟 Benefits

- **[Cost-effective scalability](ca://s?q=OrangeHRM_cost_benefits)**: Free open-source edition for startups; modular paid plans for SMBs and enterprises.  
- **[Flexibility](ca://s?q=OrangeHRM_flexibility)**: Self-hosting or cloud deployment options.  
- **[Integration-ready](ca://s?q=OrangeHRM_integrations)**: Works with ADP, Paylocity, Workable ATS, iSpring Suite, and more.  
- **[Employee engagement](ca://s?q=OrangeHRM_employee_engagement)**: Performance reviews, surveys, and career development tools foster culture.  
- **[Compliance & security](ca://s?q=OrangeHRM_security_compliance)**: GDPR-ready, audit trails, MFA, and secure hosting on AWS.  
- **[Mobile access](ca://s?q=OrangeHRM_mobile_app)**: Employees and managers can use HR functions on the go.
